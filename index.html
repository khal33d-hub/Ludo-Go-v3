How it plays

  • 3 pieces each, scattered at random 1 to 6 spaces from home. Both players start from the same spots.
  • 4 counted rolls of one die. Move one piece by the number rolled.
  • A piece needs the exact number to get home. Overshooting isn't allowed.
  • A 6 is a normal roll. It uses up one of your rolls like any other number.
  • If no piece can move, the roll is lost.
  • No killing. Pieces can share a square.
  • To win: most pieces home. If level, fewest steps left. Same again is a draw.
